## Brand & Style

This design system delivers an uncompromising, institutional-grade analytical environment engineered for statutory auditors, forensic revenue analysts, and enterprise controllers. The ethos merges the analytical density of a terminal interface with modern forensic clarity. The visual tone radiates sovereign authority, infallible data precision, and quiet computational confidence.

### Visual Style
The aesthetic employs a precision-engineered, structural-border architecture with crisp micro-radii, hyper-dense spatial metrics, and deliberate chromatic hierarchy. Surfaces stay clear of unnecessary ornamentation or heavy blur filters, relying instead on clean 1px architectural hairline dividers, disciplined tabular layouts, and high-contrast typographic weight shifts.

### Emotional Response
- **Auditor Trust & Infallibility:** Users must perceive zero ambiguity. Every number, confidence metric, and anomaly vector feels defensible before regulatory scrutiny.
- **Cognitive Calm under Density:** Immense financial volumes must remain digestible through clean vertical alignment, tabular numerals, and low visual noise.
- **Forensic Precision:** AI recommendations read not as speculative suggestions, but as deterministic, explainable mathematical proofs.

## Colors

The palette establishes an ultra-clean base with sharp functional separation between informational typography, structural borders, and forensic signals.

### Surface and Structure
- **Canvas Base (`#F8FAFC`):** Grounding light gray surface reducing eye strain across long auditing shifts.
- **Card & Cell Surface (`#FFFFFF`):** High-clarity white for active evaluation planes, metrics cards, and table rows.
- **Hairline Borders (`#E2E8F0` / `#CBD5E1`):** Structural 1px dividers establishing grid containment without visual clutter.

### Primary Typography
- **Primary Text (`#0F172A`):** Deep navy-slate providing maximum legible contrast for numerical amounts and headlines.
- **Secondary Text (`#1E293B`):** Dark slate for sub-metrics, table headers, and structural labels.
- **Muted/Caption (`#64748B`):** Neutral slate for metadata, timestamps, and column descriptors.

### Specialized AI & Behavioral Spectrum
- **AI Intelligence Blue (`#2563EB`):** Dedicated exclusively to algorithmic insights, pattern deductions, and automated recovery actions.
- **Behavioral DNA Violet (`#7C3AED`):** Applied to entity behavioral clustering, transaction fingerprinting, and audit trails.

### Audit & Risk Semantic Hierarchy
- **Critical / Confirmed Leakage (`#DC2626`):** Direct financial leakage, audit failures, and high-severity compliance flags.
- **High Risk (`#EA580C`):** Immediate exposure thresholds, severe variance, and untracked reconciliation gaps.
- **Medium Risk (`#D97706`):** Anomalous timing, marginal contract deviation, and secondary review triggers.
- **Healthy / Recovered (`#059669`):** Fully reconciled ledger line items, verified statutory remittances, and captured leakage.

## Typography

The dual-type strategy separates analytical structure from body parsing:
- **Headings & Quantitative Nodes (`IBM Plex Sans`):** Leveraged for tabular numerals, headers, key metric readouts, and metadata tags. Delivers mechanical crispness and zero character misinterpretation.
- **Narrative & Explanation (`Inter`):** Powers audit rationales, AI reasoning traces, contract text excerpts, and dense report bodies for long-form comfort.

### Tabular Formatting Rules
All currency, reconciliation balances, confidence percentage values, and transaction hash sequences must explicitly use `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums lining-nums`. Monospaced numerical glyph alignment avoids jumping baselines across vertical data tables.

## Layout & Spacing

The interface is driven by a rigid 8px baseline rhythm (with 4px sub-grid subdivisions for high-density badges, row paddings, and status chips). Content conforms to a data-dense fluid workspace that optimizes monitor real estate for deep multi-column audit pipelines.

### Workspace Structure
- **Desktop Grid (1440px+):** Fluid 24-column or 12-column analytical grid with 16px (`gutter`) gutters and 24px (`margin`) margins.
- **Dense Data Zones:** Metric groupings and side-by-side transaction reconciliation panels use 8px gaps (`space-sm`) to keep correlated items within the same visual glance.
- **Audit Canvas Flow:** Primary workspaces feature an immutable left navigation (64px collapsed, 240px expanded), an active master audit ledger view, and a right-docked expandable AI Forensic Inspector pane (380px fixed width).

## Elevation & Depth

To mimic precision physical balance sheets and high-performance financial terminals, depth is communicated through **structural 1px hairline borders and tonal surfaces** rather than dramatic shadow blur.

- **Level 0 (Base Canvas):** `#F8FAFC`. The foundational backdrop for the entire platform.
- **Level 1 (Surface Containers):** `#FFFFFF` paired with an explicit `1px solid #E2E8F0` border. No shadow. Used for data tables, audit cards, and inspection tabs.
- **Level 2 (Hover & Focus Layers):** `#FFFFFF` with `1px solid #CBD5E1` and an micro-shadow: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals & Forensic Popovers):** `#FFFFFF` with `1px solid #94A3B8` and focused elevation: `0 8px 16px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 4px 6px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Level 4 (Critical Threat Drawer):** Docked flyouts bordered with `1px solid #CBD5E1` accompanied by a directional scrim (`rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.3)`).

## Shapes

The design system standardizes on a **Soft (Level 1)** geometric language. Sharp, restrained corners underline professional rigor and maximize usable tabular real estate.

- **Standard Containers & Data Cells:** `4px` (`0.25rem`). Used across input fields, table selection boxes, data cards, and metric containers.
- **Contextual Flyouts & Modals (`rounded-lg`):** `8px` (`0.5rem`). Applied strictly to elevated surface dialogs and contextual dropdowns.
- **Confidence Indicators & Risk Pills:** Pill shape (`9999px`) reserved exclusively for micro-status indicators, confidence score indicators, and statutory state badges to cleanly contrast against rectangular data grids.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Audit Action:** Solid `#0F172A` fill, `#FFFFFF` text, `4px` corner radius, `8px 14px` padding. Subtle hover state to `#1E293B`.
- **AI Accelerated Action:** Deep blue `#2563EB` fill, `#FFFFFF` text, paired with a leading AI spark micro-icon.
- **Secondary / Actionable Row Item:** `#FFFFFF` background, `1px solid #CBD5E1`, `#1E293B` text. Active hover shifts to `#F8FAFC`.
- **Destructive / Leakage Quarantine:** Crimson `#DC2626` background, `#FFFFFF` text, used exclusively for dispute enforcement and ledger stops.

### Input Fields & Filter Controllers
- **Height & Density:** Compact 32px height standard for desktop data density.
- **Borders & Focus:** Default `1px solid #CBD5E1` on `#FFFFFF`. Focus transitions to `1px solid #2563EB` with an authoritative `0 0 0 1px #2563EB` ring (no fuzzy focus halos).
- **Embedded Currency / Unit Affixes:** Prefix/suffix currency denominations (`USD`, `EUR`, `BPS`) rendered in `#64748B` with `data-mono-sm` font sizing.

### Financial Data Tables
- **Header Rows:** 36px height, background `#F1F5F9`, typography `label-sm` uppercase tracking (`#475569`), bottom border `1px solid #CBD5E1`.
- **Data Rows:** 40px default row height (32px compact mode), background `#FFFFFF`, bottom border `1px solid #F1F5F9`. Hover state invokes `#F8FAFC` row highlight.
- **Cell Alignment:** Strict alignment rules—text aligns left; financial values, percentages, and dates align right with tabular mono typography.
- **Disputed / Flagged Row Accent:** 2px solid left border indicator corresponding to risk tier (e.g., `#DC2626` for confirmed leakage).

### Risk Badges & Status Chips
- **Structural Construction:** `18px` height, rounded pill shape, `2px 8px` padding, `label-sm` typography.
- **Critical Leakage:** Background `#FEF2F2`, text `#991B1B`, border `1px solid #F87171`.
- **High Risk:** Background `#FFF7ED`, text `#9A3412`, border `1px solid #FDBA74`.
- **Medium Risk:** Background `#FFFBEB`, text `#92400E`, border `1px solid #FCD34D`.
- **Healthy / Verified:** Background `#ECFDF5`, text `#065F46`, border `1px solid #6EE7B7`.

### AI Confidence Score Pills
- **Visual Display:** Combined score indicator containing a dual-tone fill and numerical ratio (e.g., `99.4% MATCH`).
- **Styling:** Background `#EFF6FF`, text `#1E40AF`, border `1px solid #BFDBFE`.
- **Behavioral DNA Variation:** Background `#F5F3FF`, text `#5B21B6`, border `1px solid #DDD6FE` to indicate behavioral fingerprint certainty.

### Forensic Metric & Variance Cards
- **Padding & Structure:** White `#FFFFFF` card, `1px solid #E2E8F0`, `space-lg` (16px) internal padding.
- **Header Structure:** Label in `label-sm` (`#64748B`), primary monetary volume in `data-mono-lg` (`#0F172A`), accompanied by micro variance delta badge (e.g., `-$128,400.00 (-2.4%)` in `#DC2626`).
- **Footer Metadata:** Timestamp and trace ID anchored via `data-mono-sm` in `#94A3B8`.
